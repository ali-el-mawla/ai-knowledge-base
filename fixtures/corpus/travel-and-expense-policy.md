# Travel and Expense Policy

Version 3.1, effective 1 January 2026. Owner: Finance, Controller's office. Applies to all Quaylark Systems employees.

## Purpose and principles

This policy explains what Quaylark pays for when you travel or spend money on company business, who approves it, and how you get reimbursed. The basic rule is simple: spend company money as carefully as you would spend your own, and keep the paperwork.

- All amounts in this policy are in USD. Expenses in other currencies are converted at the rate shown on the card statement, or at the published rate on the transaction date for out-of-pocket spending.
- Book flights, rail and hotels through the Quaylark Travel Desk in the expense system unless the Travel Desk cannot offer the trip.
- Anything not covered here needs written approval from the Controller before you spend.

## Before you travel

### Pre-approval

- Domestic trips need manager approval in the expense system before booking.
- International travel needs pre-approval from your manager and your department VP at least 14 days before departure.
- Trips for customer on-site work must include the customer's account ID in the trip request, so the cost can be charged to the right project.

### Booking rules

- Flights shorter than 6 hours are booked in economy class.
- Premium economy is allowed for flights of 6 hours or longer.
- Business class is allowed only for flights longer than 10 hours, and only with VP approval.
- Book at least 14 days ahead when you can. Last-minute bookings need a short reason in the request.
- Rail is preferred over flying for trips under 4 hours, for example Rotterdam to Brussels or Paris.
- Hotels must stay within the nightly lodging cap for the region (see the table below).
- Rental cars are intermediate class or smaller. In Canada and the United States, decline the extra collision insurance because the corporate policy already covers it.

## Per diems and lodging caps by region

Meals are covered by a daily per diem instead of receipts. Hotels are reimbursed at cost, up to the nightly cap for the region.

| Region tier | Example cities                                            | Daily meal per diem | Nightly lodging cap |
| ----------- | --------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------- | ------------------- |
| Tier A      | New York, San Francisco, London, Zurich, Singapore, Tokyo | 95 USD              | 320 USD             |
| Tier B      | Toronto, Rotterdam, Amsterdam, Chicago, Berlin, Sydney    | 75 USD              | 230 USD             |
| Tier C      | All other cities                                          | 55 USD              | 160 USD             |

- The per diem covers meals, snacks, coffee and tips. Claim it under EXP-TRV-04. No receipts are needed.
- The day you leave and the day you return are paid at 75% of the daily rate.
- If a meal is provided (for example by a conference or a customer), deduct 20% of the daily rate for breakfast, 30% for lunch and 50% for dinner.
- You cannot claim a per diem in the city where your home office is.
- If no hotel within the cap is available, the Travel Desk can approve up to 25% above the cap and records the reason.

## Approval thresholds

Approval is based on the total of a single expense report.

- Up to 250 USD: approved automatically when every receipt is attached. Finance audits a random sample each month.
- Above 250 USD and up to 2,500 USD: your direct manager.
- Above 2,500 USD and up to 10,000 USD: your department VP.
- Above 10,000 USD: the Chief Financial Officer.
- Nobody may approve their own expenses, or the expenses of the person they report to. Those reports go one level higher.

## Meals and entertainment

### Client entertainment

Client entertainment must have a clear business purpose. Record the names and companies of everyone who attended. The most senior Quaylark employee present pays and submits the claim under EXP-ENT-01.

### Limits

- 120 USD per attendee, including tax and tip.
- No more than 2 alcoholic drinks per person, and alcohol only with a meal.
- Gifts to a customer contact are capped at 75 USD per recipient per calendar year.
- No gifts or hospitality of any value for government officials.
- Meals with other Quaylark employees during a trip are covered by the per diem, not by entertainment.

## Corporate cards

- Corporate cards are issued after 60 days of employment to employees who travel at least 4 times a year.
- The standard monthly card limit is 5,000 USD. The Controller can raise it to 15,000 USD for a specific trip or event.
- Personal spending on the card is not allowed. If it happens by mistake, repay it within 10 working days and tell Finance.
- Card transactions must be matched with receipts and codes within 30 days of the statement date.

## Submitting expenses

- Submit expenses within 30 days of the end of the trip or the date of purchase.
- Claims submitted more than 90 days after the expense are not reimbursed.
- A receipt is required for any single item of 25 USD or more, except per diems.
- Reimbursement is paid with the next payroll run after approval, usually within 10 working days.
- If a receipt is lost, fill in the missing receipt declaration, form FIN-MRD-01. You can use it at most 2 times per calendar year.

## Receipt retention

Keep original paper receipts for 90 days after you are reimbursed, in case they are selected for audit. Finance keeps digital copies of receipts and expense reports for 7 years after the end of the fiscal year, as set out in the Data Retention and Privacy Policy.

## Disputes and escalation

If an expense is rejected, first talk to the approver who rejected it. If you still disagree, escalate to the Finance Controller within 10 working days of the rejection. The Controller's decision is final. Suspected misuse of company money should be reported to the ethics hotline.

## Non-reimbursable items

The following are never reimbursed:

- Seat or cabin upgrades that the booking rules do not allow
- Hotel minibar, in-room movies and spa services
- Traffic and parking fines
- Airline lounge memberships
- Personal travel insurance, passports and visas for personal trips
- Travel costs for family members or partners
- Expenses for anyone who is not a Quaylark employee, except client entertainment
