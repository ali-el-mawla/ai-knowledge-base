# Quaylark Systems Employee Handbook

Version 4.2, effective 1 February 2026. Owner: People Operations.

## About this handbook

This handbook applies to all full-time and part-time employees of Quaylark Systems, Inc. and its subsidiaries in Canada, the Netherlands and Singapore. Contractors follow the terms of their own agreements. Where local employment law gives an employee more generous terms than this handbook, local law applies.

Quaylark has about 430 employees across three offices: Toronto (headquarters), Rotterdam and Singapore. Questions about anything in this handbook go to your People Partner or to the #ask-people channel.

## Working hours

### Standard week

The standard working week is 40 hours in Toronto and Singapore and 38 hours in Rotterdam. Part-time schedules are agreed in writing with your manager and People Operations.

### Core hours

Everyone is expected to be available between 10:00 and 15:00 in their local time zone, Monday to Friday. Outside core hours you can arrange your day as you and your manager agree, as long as meetings with other offices are covered.

### Overtime

Employees who are eligible for overtime must get written approval from their manager before working more than 44 hours in a week. Approved overtime is paid at 1.5 times the regular hourly rate, or taken as time off in lieu within 60 days.

### On-call allowance

Engineers on the production on-call rotation receive an on-call allowance of 250 USD per week as primary and 125 USD per week as secondary, paid through payroll in local currency. How the rotation works is described in the Information Security and Incident Response Runbook.

### Annual leave

- Full-time employees receive 22 working days of paid annual leave per calendar year, accruing at 1.83 days per month.
- From the start of the fifth year of service, the entitlement rises to 25 working days.
- Up to 5 unused days can be carried over into the next year. Carried-over days must be used by 31 March, after which they expire unless local law says otherwise.
- Public holidays follow the calendar of your office and do not count against annual leave.
- All offices close for Quiet Week, from 24 December to 1 January. Quiet Week days are given in addition to annual leave.
- Part-time employees receive annual leave in proportion to their contracted hours.

### Sick leave

- Everyone receives 10 paid sick days per calendar year. Unused sick days do not carry over.
- Tell your manager before 10:00 local time on the first day of absence.
- A medical certificate is required after 3 consecutive sick days.
- For longer illness, short-term disability insurance pays 70% of base salary for up to 26 weeks, starting after the paid sick days are used, where local law does not already provide this.

### Parental leave

Employees become eligible for parental leave after 90 days of employment. Parental leave applies to birth, adoption and surrogacy.

- Primary caregiver: 18 weeks at full base pay.
- Secondary caregiver: 6 weeks at full base pay.
- Leave must be taken within 12 months of the birth or placement and can be split into up to 3 blocks.
- On return, employees can work a phased return of 4 weeks at 80% of their normal hours on full pay.
- Statutory parental benefits are topped up by Quaylark so that the total never exceeds full base pay.

## Remote and hybrid work

### Work arrangements

Quaylark has three work arrangements: office-based, hybrid and remote.

- Hybrid employees who live within 50 km of an office come in on anchor days, which are Tuesday and Thursday.
- Remote status needs approval from the department VP and People Operations, and is reviewed once a year.
- Office-based roles are those that need on-site hardware or lab access.

### Home office support

- A one-time home office stipend of 600 USD, claimed under expense code EXP-EQP-02 within the first 90 days of employment.
- A monthly internet allowance of 45 USD, paid through payroll.
- A company laptop and a hardware security key, returned within 10 working days of your last day.

### Working from abroad

You may work from a country other than your country of employment for up to 30 days in any rolling 12-month period. Submit form HR-WFA-02 at least 15 working days before you travel. People Operations checks tax and immigration rules for the destination, and some countries are excluded for export control reasons. The current list is on the intranet.

### Security when working remotely

- Use the always-on VPN for any access to production or customer data.
- Never work on customer data from a shared or public computer.
- Lock your screen whenever you step away. Screens lock automatically after 5 minutes.
- Report a lost or stolen laptop or phone to the Security team within 1 hour at security@quaylark.example.

## Employee retention and growth

Quaylark wants people to build a long career here. The programs below are the main tools People Operations uses for employee retention.

### Stay interviews

Managers hold a stay interview with each direct report twice a year, in April and October. Stay interviews are separate from performance reviews and focus on what keeps the person at Quaylark and what might make them leave.

### Retention bonuses

In limited cases the company offers a retention bonus to keep a key person through a critical project or an acquisition. Retention bonuses are approved by the department VP and the Chief People Officer.

- If the employee resigns within 12 months of receiving a retention bonus, they repay 100% of the gross amount.
- If they resign between 12 and 18 months after receiving it, they repay 50%.
- No repayment is due if the company ends the employment without cause.

### Sabbatical

After 5 years of continuous service, employees can take a 4-week paid sabbatical. It must be taken within 24 months of becoming eligible and planned with your manager at least 3 months ahead.

### Learning budget

Every employee has a learning budget of 1,200 USD per calendar year for courses, books and conferences, claimed under expense code EXP-TRN-01. Unused budget does not carry over.

### Performance reviews

Formal performance reviews happen once a year in January, with a lighter mid-year check-in in July. Promotions are decided in the January cycle.

## Raising concerns and escalation

If you have a workplace concern, raise it with your manager first. If it is not resolved within 10 working days, escalate it to your People Partner. If it is still not resolved, or if it involves your People Partner, escalate it to the Chief People Officer.

Concerns about fraud, harassment or safety can go straight to the anonymous ethics hotline, which is available 24 hours a day. Quaylark does not tolerate retaliation against anyone who raises a concern in good faith.

## Probation and notice

- New employees serve a probation period of 90 days.
- During probation, the notice period on either side is 1 week.
- After probation, the notice period is 4 weeks in Canada and Singapore. In the Netherlands, notice periods follow Dutch law and the employment contract.
- Leavers hand back all equipment within 10 working days and complete an exit interview with People Operations.
