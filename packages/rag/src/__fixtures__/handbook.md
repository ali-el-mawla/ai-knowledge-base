# Employee handbook

## Leave policy

### Annual leave

Full-time employees get 25 days of paid annual leave per calendar year. Unused days carry over until 31 March of the next year.

### Parental leave

Birth parents get 16 weeks of fully paid leave. Other parents get 8 weeks.

- Leave can start up to 4 weeks before the due date.
- It can be split into two blocks.

## Expenses

### Travel

Book travel through the portal. Economy class for flights under 6 hours.

| Item             | Limit   | Code      |
| ---------------- | ------- | --------- |
| Hotel, per night | 180 EUR | EXP-HOTEL |
| Meals, per day   | 45 EUR  | EXP-MEAL  |

### Submitting a claim

Run the CLI from the finance repository:

```bash
npx expenses submit --receipt ./receipt.pdf --code EXP-MEAL
```

Claims are paid with the next payroll.
